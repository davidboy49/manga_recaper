Above the castle, winged monsters circled.  A massive hole had been torn through the wall. The hero Vermouth and his four comrades in arms had entered the Demon Realm, Helmud, to defeat the last Demon Kings.
The heroes ran through the corridor. They had already defeated three of the five Kings, and now only two remained. But the demons blocked their path, forming an innumerable army in front of them.
Vermouth grabbed his sword and lunged forward. His weapon glowed brightly, and a powerful explosion erupted as his eyes shone with light.
The Great Vermouth.
The witch followed behind him, running incredibly fast with a smile on her face. Her magic would be perfect against so many monsters. Her staff glowed with a brilliant light, and a huge flame appeared in front of her. She smiled proudly at what she had done.
Wise Senya.
The Priestess stood nearby, her hands radiating light as she blessed the fallen souls. A demon attacked her from behind, but she cut off its head in a single move and looked pleased with the result.
Anise.
The headless monster collapsed to the ground. Then the man raised his axe and chopped down several enemies with one blow. Their remains flew all over the hall, and he grinned as he looked at the scene.
Fearless.
The redhead ran past him, and the man screamed, cursing his comrade and telling him to stop getting in front of him. Countless enemies stood ahead, and his eyes were filled with rage. The redhead charged toward them and unleashed a series of quick attacks, glaring at them angrily.
Reckless Hamel.
Then he put his weapon back in its sheath. Hamel stood behind the blonde. He had always envied Vermouth and always called the hero his rival, but no one ever took those words seriously.
The group stood in the middle of the hall. Hamel looked around and thought there were not that many enemies left. Suddenly, he called out to the blonde. Something was approaching him, but Hamel covered him with his own body.
Blood spilled from his mouth. The demon laughed as it looked at them. A large wound had opened in Hamel’s chest, and he fell to the ground. The monster prepared another attack, stretching its arm forward, but Vermouth managed to dodge. He closed the distance, lunged with his weapon, and cut the demon in half. Blood splashed everywhere.
The others ran to the wounded man. Vermouth put his weapon away and stood with his head lowered. Hamel should not die like this.
But Hamel smiled. This was a dignified death. In the final moments of his life, he deeply regretted that he had never been sincere with his comrades. He extended a trembling hand and told them that they should kill them all.
His friend grabbed his hand and looked into his eyes, swearing in the name of the hero that he would destroy all the Demon Kings. Hamel was glad to hear it.
The Priestess folded her hands, and Hamel asked her to pray for him. The warrior looked at him with annoyance. The dying man said that he was the bravest of them all. He knew Senya was crying, so he looked at her, about to tell the truth.
Then a leg appeared.
Eugene stomped on the book, shouting that he never liked it. He never said that. He clenched his fist. No matter how many times he read it, he started shaking every single time. Who wrote this book? If he found out who the author was, he would beat them up.
He casually picked up the book. Moreover, they always gave the others cool nicknames. Great, Wise, Holy, Fearless. But why did they call Hamel “the Foolish”? Was this written by someone he beat up three hundred years ago?
Eugene threw the book behind his back. The same went for his team. Why did they turn a blind eye to such a stupid book? They did not even bother to defend the honor of their brother in arms.
He remembered the death scene and doubted that they had ever considered the possibility of the hero’s reincarnation with all his memories from the past.
Eugene frowned. He was Hamel, who had died three hundred years ago during the war against the five Demon Kings.
Then he looked away. There was a painting on the wall depicting a warrior with a sword.
Reincarnation in the body of one of the hero’s descendants.
Eugene, thirteen years old.
